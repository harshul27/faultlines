        Data format
          
        One row (CSV) or object (JSON) per on-ball event, in time order. Coordinates are 0-100 in the acting team's attacking direction: x = 0 its own goal line, y = 0 its left touchline.

        What the terms mean
        
    
        
      
        Fault line
        The player whose absence would cost teammates the most passing connections over the last 15 minutes.
        
      
        Network dependence
        That cost as a percentage. It shows how much play depends on someone now, not certain damage: teams can re-route the ball.
        
      
        Passing spread
        How evenly the ball is shared. Lower means play runs through fewer links.
        
      
        Progressive pass
        A completed pass that moves the ball at least about 10 m towards goal.
        
      
        Plan fit
        0-100: how a player is doing on what your game plan asks of their position. 50 = normal for the position.
        
      
        Load vs plan
        A player's share of the team's actions against what their position and your plan imply. Overloaded = the team leans on them more than intended.
        
      
        Expected points added
        Chances created or taken, weighted by how many points a goal was worth at that moment.
        
      
        Cues
        Broadcast-style alerts when something unusual for the league happens.
